科技前沿

Artificial Intelligence is changing our world in many ways. AI robots can now help doctors perform surgeries, assist scientists in discovering new medicines, and even drive cars without human help. Smart homes use AI to control lights, temperature, and security systems. In the future, AI may help us solve big problems like climate change and find cures for diseases. However, we must use AI responsibly to make sure it helps everyone.

人工智能正以多种方式改变着我们的世界。AI机器人现在可以帮助医生做手术，帮助科学家发现新药，甚至无人驾驶汽车。智能家居使用AI来控制灯光、温度和安保系统。未来，AI可能帮助我们解决气候变化等大问题，找到疾病的治疗方法。然而，我们必须负责任地使用AI，确保它能帮助每一个人。

📖 语法讲解 - 情态动词can/may

📐 规则说明
  • can 表示"能，会"表示能力或可能性
  • may 表示"可以，可能"表示允许或可能性
  • 否定形式：cannot (can't) / may not
  • 后面加动词原形
💡 例句

AI robots can help doctors perform surgeries.
Smart homes may use AI to control lights.
We must use AI responsibly.

❓ 阅读理解 (4题)

1. What can AI robots do according to the passage?
2. What may AI help us solve in the future?
